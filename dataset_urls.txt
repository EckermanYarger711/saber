Saber external resource register
================================
Every address below was fetched from this host on 2026-09-29 and the returned body was
inspected against the row the article's Supplementary Table A1 prints for that resource.
The registry rows name a version, an accession or a URL, a licence and an access date; this
file records the address that was actually reachable and what the fetch returned.

The primary section holds the resources whose returned content matched the registry row. The
secondary section holds two accessions the registry carries whose record could not be
matched from this host, because the archive answers unattended clients with an interstitial
challenge page; their addresses are recorded with that outcome rather than dropped.

Nothing here is redistributed with the release. No dataset is bundled, and no gated model
weight is linked.
