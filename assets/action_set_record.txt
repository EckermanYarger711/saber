Reference protocol and action-set record
========================================
Recorded for the release's own use; written by build_context from
configs/dish/three_channel_6h.yaml and configs/panel/five_corpus.yaml.

Action set of Eq. (1)
---------------------
fields        24 wells x 8 fields of view
channels      3
exposures     4
axial depths  3
fluidics      2
doses         6, spanning the vehicle and the 1e-10 to 1e-4 M range of Table A1

Reference protocol
------------------
One field per well, on the first three channels, at a single exposure, in focus,
without a medium exchange and without a dose. The damage of that frame set, summed
over a session's decisions, is the budget D of Sec. 2.4; the fixed-cadence policy
of Table 1 executes exactly that set, which is why its budget usage is total.

Session
-------
One decision per (well, epoch) pair over the wells the run monitors, at the
reference cadence of six hours per epoch. The executed scale is recorded in
verification_report.json under "session" and "scale".
